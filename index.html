What happens after submission?

1. Barangay staff checks your request.
2. Staff may contact you if information is incomplete.
3. Once verified, the request is marked ready for pick-up.
4. Bring your valid ID and claim the document at the barangay hall.

About BarangayGO

BArangayGO is a web-based barangay portal for Barangay Palico II, City of Imus, Cavite, designed to make document requests more organized and convenient for residents. The project focuses on two main services: Barangay Clearance and Certificate of Indigency.

Instead of starting the process only at the barangay hall, residents can use the portal to review requirements, submit their request information, and check announcements before going to the office for verification and document claiming.

1
Request OnlineStart the document request through the portal.
2
Check RequirementsSee what to prepare before visiting the office.
3
Claim at the OfficeComplete verification and claim the requested document.
